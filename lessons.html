Guitar Anatomy

Proper Positioning

📥 Download Guitar Basics (PDF)

Overview Details

1. Main Guitar Parts

  • Headstock: Top section holding the tuning pegs.
  • Tuning Pegs: Knobs turned to adjust string pitch.
  • Nut: Small ridge guiding strings into the neck.
  • Neck / Fretboard: Wood neck containing metal frets to adjust pitch.
  • Body: Amplifies sound output.
  • Bridge: Anchors strings to the body.

2. Correct Posture

  • Body: Sit straight up; rest guitar curve on right thigh.
  • Alignment: Keep back of guitar flush to your chest.
  • Fretting Hand: Thumb rests behind neck, press strings with fingertips.
  • Strumming Hand: Forearm rests on top edge, loose wrist over soundhole.

3. String Tuning Names (6th to 1st)

  • 6 (Thickest / Low E): E
  • 5: A | 4: D | 3: G | 2: B
  • 1 (Thinnest / High E): E

Strumming Technique

Overview Details

1. Holding the Pick

  • Pinch pick between thumb and side of index finger (~0.5cm tip exposed).
  • Keep grip firm enough to prevent slipping, but loose enough to glide smoothly.

2. Strumming Motion

  • Wrist Rotation: Motion comes from a relaxed wrist, not a rigid arm.
  • Continuous Motion: Keep arm moving rhythmically up and down.

3. Common Patterns (4/4 Time)

  • All-Downs (1 - 2 - 3 - 4): Down, Down, Down, Down
  • Alternating (1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &): Down-Up, Down-Up, Down-Up, Down-Up
  • Pop/Folk Rhythm: Down, Down-Up, (miss), Up-Down-Up

💡 Pro-tip: Keep continuous wrist momentum even during silent miss-strums!

Overview Details

1. Finger Numbering Guide

  • 1: Index Finger | 2: Middle Finger | 3: Ring Finger | 4: Pinky Finger

2. Essential Chord Shapes

  • E Minor (Em): Finger 2 on 5th string (Fret 2) | Finger 3 on 4th string (Fret 2)
  • A Minor (Am): Finger 1 on 2nd string (Fret 1) | Finger 2 on 4th string (Fret 2) | Finger 3 on 3rd string (Fret 2)
  • C Major (C): Finger 1 on 2nd string (Fret 1) | Finger 2 on 4th string (Fret 2) | Finger 3 on 5th string (Fret 3)
  • G Major (G): Finger 2 on 5th string (Fret 2) | Finger 3 on 6th string (Fret 3) | Finger 4 on 1st string (Fret 3)
  • D Major (D): Finger 1 on 3rd string (Fret 2) | Finger 2 on 1st string (Fret 2) | Finger 3 on 2nd string (Fret 3)

💡 Touch to reveal tip: Press down firmly using fingertips near the frets for clear notes!
